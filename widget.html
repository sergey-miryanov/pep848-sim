Widget stand-in

The first widget offers two workloads through its select. The stand-in, close to prototype #6's, runs both collectors at the PEP's thresholds over 8 MB of module globals, a 20 MB tree rebuilt every 80 MB, and churn with 3% of its allocations in cycles. It is no preset, and it goes once presets take its place. The create_gc_cycles entry names its preset in place of a workload and a run length; the worker sends the preset back spelled out, and the entry's control edits what it sent.

Each widget shows a few controls. Moving one changes only the value it shows; releasing it starts that widget's runs again, and the other widget's runs go on.

The second widget runs the workload tests/test_run.py pins: a 20 MB run with threshold0 at 20 kB, 1 MB of module globals, and a 100 kB tree rebuilt every 1 MB.
