Presets against their benchmarks

Each preset models one worker of a pyperformance benchmark, from start-up to shutdown. gcmon traced 20 workers of each benchmark on the reference branch, and this page draws them beside the preset's run, a collection at a time: what each collection examined and freed, its generation's yield so far, and the work an old collection did. Where the bold line leaves the faint ones, the preset drifts from its benchmark.

A point stands at the MB its worker had allocated when the collection started, on the clock the simulator runs on, so a run that ends early stops short of its workers. Counts are real objects.
