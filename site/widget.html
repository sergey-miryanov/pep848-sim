Widget stand-in

A widget runs a stand-in workload, close to prototype #6's, with both collectors at the PEP's thresholds: 8 MB of module globals, a 20 MB tree rebuilt every 80 MB, and churn with 3% of its allocations in cycles. The stand-in is no preset, and it goes when the first preset arrives.
